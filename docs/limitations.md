# Limitations

Keep this file honest and update it every round. Judges reward clear limits more than inflated results.

## Known from the start
1. **Physics mismatch:** Earth cannot reproduce lunar vacuum or permanently shadowed region temperatures. Scores mean "best terrestrial proxy for the listed factors" and nothing more.
2. **Resolution:** a coarse global grid averages out small features. It is a screening step and not a site survey.
3. **Small label set:** known analog lists are short. Validation results carry wide uncertainty.
4. **Subjective targets:** target values come from literature and involve judgment. Show sensitivity to them.
5. **Data gaps:** polar regions and some tiles may be missing or filled in. Document every fill.
6. **Novelty claim:** "new location" only means not in our reference list. It does not mean never studied.
